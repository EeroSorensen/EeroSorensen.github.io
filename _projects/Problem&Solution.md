## Overview ##

This project presents the design and purpoe of an app focused on improving aerobic capacity in individuals with Down syndrome. The app uses evidence based exercise stategies to provide practical tools and goals to support physical activity. Emphasizing user-friendly navigation it tailors engaging content for indviduals to interact with. Drawing on principles used in literature, it demonstrates how structured, enjoyable and supportive resources can promote long-term health, independence and quality of life.
